# Blind outreach quality rubric

The final selector sees only recipient title, public seller description, subject, and body. It must not see the internal hypothesis, account brief, evidence score, generator score, or critic history. Review candidates unchanged; never merge or rewrite them.

`none_sendable` is a valid and desirable result.

## Weighted quality

- Recipient value: 30
- Specificity: 20
- Credibility: 20
- Reply ease: 15
- Naturalness: 15

Require 80/100 and no hard failure. Two independent selectors must choose the same unchanged candidate; disagreement holds the account for human review.

## Hard failures for every brand

- The message mainly asks the recipient to educate the sender.
- It contains no concrete prepared or honestly producible deliverable.
- Hidden brief context is required to understand why it matters.
- It is accurate but commercially uninteresting.
- It asks for a meeting before earning interest.
- It contains unsupported facts, ownership, urgency, results, ROI, or proof.
- It could be sent unchanged to several accounts.
- It has no documented, approved consent basis.

## OutageHub

The email must improve or test one decision: diagnose, dispatch, escalate, notify, investigate, place a generator, attribute downtime, or wait for restoration. It must use one selected play and provide a location-specific result, a specific producible comparison/sample, or an easy correction/routing path. Reject product-first API descriptions, generic company categories, sender-benefit closers, repeated defensive caveats, and cross-account template reuse.

## Wapahki

The first lines must establish one verified facility fact and bounded physical task. The message must contribute a real Task Brief, completed screen, task-map observation, technical distinction, or one precise question answerable from memory. Reject generic automation pitches, invented fit/ROI/performance, research-memo language, mandatory credential furniture, or mention of an artifact that does not exist.

## Batch review

Compare the entire current generation batch before selection. Block excessive phrase, opening, sentence-structure, question, and CTA similarity. A batch that passes one-by-one but reads like mail merge fails as a batch.

## Pilot release bars

- OutageHub: at least 20 real messages pass current deterministic policy, blind review, exact-copy manual approval, and controlled SMTP delivery.
- Wapahki: at least 10 real Task Briefs and 10 unchanged messages pass the same controls before any external delivery.

The target is positive replies, correct referrals, artifact requests, qualified task conversations, and opportunities—not opens or model scores.
