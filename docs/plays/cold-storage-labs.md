# OutageHub play: cold storage and laboratories

## Job and decision

Prioritize locations with time-sensitive backup-power exposure and distinguish a utility event from a site fault before escalation or continuity action.

## Likely people

Facilities, reliability, maintenance, business continuity, lab operations, and cold-chain operations.

## Useful first contributions

A location-specific historical comparison, a public-site monitoring prototype, or a decision map that shows where utility status could enter an existing alarm workflow.

## Evaluation and disqualification

Never infer product loss, temperature excursion, generator behavior, or regulatory impact. Test whether the recipient owns the alarm decision and whether utility context arrives soon enough to change it. Hold generic category summaries.
