# OutageHub play: generator dispatch

## Job and decision

Prioritize which sites may require temporary power, when to dispatch, and when restoration evidence makes waiting or redeployment more appropriate.

## Likely people

Dispatch operations, fleet operations, temporary-power service, field operations, and emergency-response coordination.

## Useful first contributions

A public-site coverage sample, a location-specific historical restoration replay, or a dispatch-priority input sketch. Do not claim avoided dispatches or fuel savings without customer data.

## Evaluation and disqualification

Test utility coverage, restoration timing, site criticality inputs, and the human decision point. Disqualify if utility data cannot arrive before dispatch, asset locations are unavailable, or dispatch decisions are unrelated to public utility conditions.
