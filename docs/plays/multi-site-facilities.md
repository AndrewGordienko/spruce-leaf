# OutageHub play: multi-site facilities

## Job and decision

Identify which locations are likely affected by a utility outage without checking many utility maps, then prioritize investigation, notification, continuity actions, or waiting.

## Likely people

Facilities operations, reliability, business continuity, security operations, property operations, and portfolio systems owners.

## Strong signals

- several public Canadian addresses;
- centralized facilities monitoring or continuity responsibility;
- a current portfolio expansion, resilience initiative, or public historical event.

## Useful first contributions

- a portfolio outage snapshot for public sites;
- a historical replay for one or three locations;
- a coverage table showing supported utilities and address quality;
- a notification and escalation decision sketch.

## Easy questions

- When several site alarms arrive, is utility status checked centrally or by each location?
- Is that decision owned by facilities operations or business continuity?

## Evaluation and disqualification

Test address coverage, who owns portfolio monitoring, and whether utility context changes prioritization. Disqualify when the portfolio lacks centralized decisions, location data is unusable, or existing building systems already provide reliable utility attribution.
