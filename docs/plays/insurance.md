# OutageHub play: insurance

## Job and decision

Verify location-level outage timing, utility, and duration as one input to claims, product, underwriting, or data decisions.

## Likely people

Claims operations, claims product, catastrophe/data teams, fraud or investigation teams, and external-data owners.

## Useful first contributions

A historical event extract for one public location, a sample API response, a coverage assessment, or a cited event timeline. OutageHub evidence is context, not proof of loss or causation.

## Evaluation and disqualification

Test location resolution, historical depth, provenance, and the exact decision improved. Disqualify when utility timing cannot support the required standard, the person cannot observe or route the work, or the proposed use would overstate causation.
