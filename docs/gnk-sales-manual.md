# GnK sales manual v0.1

Status: working doctrine for founder-led, manually supervised selling
Motion: triggered account → expensive workflow → accountable person → bounded contribution → useful reply → diagnosis → paid work

Core doctrine: GnK does not sell developers, AI, or generic custom software. It removes one expensive operational or technical constraint with a bounded, testable piece of work.

This manual is a copy-policy input. Research, opportunity qualification, offer selection, writing, review, follow-up, pipeline stages, and outcome learning must agree with it.

## Public foundations

- [GitLab's Sales Development handbook](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/marketing/sales-development/) gives its highest account priority to strong ICP fit with a specific, time-bound trigger. GnK is stricter: only triggered Tier 1 accounts receive handcrafted cold outreach.
- [Founding Sales](https://www.foundingsales.com/) treats founder-led sales as discovering and refining the initial sales motion before handing it to a team. Its [prospecting chapter](https://www.foundingsales.com/4-prospecting) emphasizes observable demand signifiers and demand magnitude, not merely available contacts.
- [Heavybit's Enterprise Sales for Technical Founders](https://www.heavybit.com/library/video/enterprise-sales-for-technical-founders) reinforces founder-led experimentation, learning value and pricing, and asking more about customers instead of overexplaining technology.
- [Gong's executive cold-email analysis](https://www.gong.io/blog/do-execs-really-reply-to-cold-email-here-s-what-the-data-says) supports concise customer-language notes and a concrete offer instead of a reflexive meeting request.
- [Winning by Design's SPICED framework](https://winningbydesign.com/resources/blueprints/the-spiced-framework/) organizes post-engagement diagnosis around Situation, Pain, Impact, Critical Event, and Decision. These are discovery fields, not questions to stuff into a cold email.
- [GitLab's opportunity-stage guidance](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/commercial/comm-sales-opp-stages/) uses explicit activities and exit criteria. GnK advances a stage only after a buyer action supplies its exit evidence.
- [Hinge's professional-services nurture guide](https://hingemarketing.com/library/article/the_lead_nurturing_guide) treats education, expertise, and trust as central to complex professional-services selling. Nurture must add useful thinking, not repeat “checking in.”

These sources guide process. They do not establish GnK proof, customers, or results.

## Principles

1. **Research disqualifies.** It is not decorative personalization. Hold accounts without a current trigger, expensive workflow, accountable or routable person, and a useful supportable contribution.
2. **One opportunity equals company × trigger × workflow × person × offer.** Each element has its own evidence. A company fact cannot prove a person's responsibility.
3. **Cold outreach sells one small next step.** It does not sell a CAD $40,000–$60,000 sprint, propose architecture, or run a discovery interview by email.
4. **Give before substantial ask.** The recipient should receive a sharp outside observation, a bounded work product, or a correction/routing path that earns its place.
5. **Diagnosis follows engagement.** SPICED fields begin only after a human reply. Silence does not confirm pain or permit a larger ask.
6. **Follow-up requires a new reason to respond.** No new evidence, proof, artifact, buyer action, or routing reason means no follow-up.
7. **Founder-led until proven.** Andrew inspects prospects, copy, objections, value, pricing, delivery, and outcomes manually. Scale only a motion that has produced qualified conversations and paid work.

## Acquisition source and first contact

Spruce Leaf currently realizes acquisition through email and LinkedIn, using the governed [channel doctrine](acquisition-channels.md). Cold research, a conference interaction, webinar behavior, a referral, a partner introduction, and an existing-customer relationship are different contexts and never share a generic opener. Conference and webinar records preserve actual event behavior; source never substitutes for fit. One relationship owner remains accountable after the first reply.

The GnK customer journey is account identified → first contact → problem confirmed → application discovery → technical/commercial design → paid engagement/deployment → expansion. These are buyer-evidence stages, not seven cold messages.

## Required Sendable Brief

Writing is forbidden until a typed prospect record contains:

- verified, current trigger and source;
- one recognizable expensive workflow or technical constraint;
- person-specific responsibility evidence, or an honest adjacent/router classification;
- consequence hypothesis, clearly bounded as an inference unless confirmed;
- concrete GnK contribution;
- recipient-side offer and why it could be useful;
- credibility basis that can be stated without fabricated proof;
- smallest appropriate ask and expected response;
- required buyer input, if any;
- decision the contribution improves;
- uncertainties that must not become facts;
- documented consent basis and evidence.

The writer never sees the old account hypothesis or unrelated facts. If GnK cannot construct a supportable offer before prose, abstain.

## Offer system

Only select an offer GnK can truthfully prepare and deliver.

### Outside-in workflow risk map

Use public evidence to map one visible workflow, likely record handoffs, explicit unknowns, and the technical or operational conditions that would invalidate the opportunity. Return a cited, bounded outside view—not a claim about internal systems.

### Observable failure-surface teardown

Examine one public backend, integration, case-handling, evidence, or operational failure surface and return what is observable, what cannot be known outside-in, and one proposed validation path. Never imply access or technical findings that do not exist.

### Historical-case review

Available only after the buyer confirms the workflow and provides a small agreed sample. Reconstruct one completed claim, deduction, project event, or exception into a cited timeline/chronology with explicit success criteria.

### Paid Backend Risk and Stabilization Sprint

Available only after qualification. Use the offer defined in `businesses/gnk.toml`; do not pitch its price or scope in cold outreach.

Every selected offer states what GnK examines, what it returns, the decision improved, minimum buyer input, credibility basis, and kill conditions.

## Outreach

T1 is approximately 60–110 words with a 3–9 word operational subject. It uses one verified trigger, one recognizable expensive moment, a pointed but bounded hypothesis, one concrete contribution, and one easy question. It does not lead with GnK, custom software, AI, a platform, or ROI. Discovery-ready and routing contacts are never asked for a call.

The recipient owes Andrew no market research. Reject “trying to understand,” “would value your perspective,” generic capability paragraphs, and a diagnostic question followed by vague software language. An easy correction is legitimate only when the trigger and contribution already make the note worth reading.

Generate exactly three unchanged modes, fact-check them, and let two blind selectors choose or hold. Reviewers do not rewrite or merge. Exact-copy human approval remains mandatory.

## Follow-up

Cold planning produces T1 only. A reply, buyer action, or new current evidence may authorize a separately reviewed next touch. The absolute maximum is four touches over roughly 18 days:

1. **Day 0 — first email:** trigger, operating moment, bounded hypothesis, concrete offer, easy reply.
2. **Around day 4 — evidence:** genuinely new evidence, a useful distinction, or a preview/completed part of promised work.
3. **Around day 10 — human routing:** concise email or LinkedIn note asking whether this belongs with the recipient or a named function; do not repeat the pitch.
4. **Around day 18 — close:** close without guilt or false scarcity; move to nurture until a new trigger.

Every follow-up stores its new evidence/artifact ID or the buyer action it answers. If no new item exists, stop early. Silence alone never authorizes T2.

## Discovery and SPICED

Create SPICED records only after a human reply:

- Situation: confirmed current context and workflow.
- Pain: concrete friction expressed or confirmed by the buyer.
- Impact: consequence and magnitude, without model estimates.
- Critical event: real deadline, trigger, or reason now.
- Decision: stakeholders, criteria, process, and evidence required.

Diagnose before proposing. Establish pain, impact, budget path, decision path, implementation conditions, and technical boundaries before giving away architecture or producing a proposal.

After a real reply, create an application brief containing the current operating problem and approach, why it fails, affected people, GnK's proposed role, technology options, why a custom solution is justified, systems/data, success measures, customer inputs, first paid scope, owners, constraints, and expansion path. GnK stays technology-agnostic until the application evidence supports a choice.

## Buyer-based pipeline stages

- **Target:** trigger, workflow, and evidence meet Tier 1 policy.
- **Contact ready:** supported person, offer, brief, consent record, blind review, and exact-copy approval exist.
- **Engaged:** a human reply confirms, corrects, routes, or requests the contribution.
- **Discovery confirmed:** the buyer supplies enough SPICED evidence to define the constraint and consequence.
- **Offer qualified:** buyer and GnK agree on bounded work, inputs, decision, success criteria, and owner.
- **Proposal:** the buyer requests or accepts a commercial proposal and the decision path is known.
- **Paid:** invoice/payment evidence exists.
- **Delivery / expansion:** the agreed result is delivered and any adjacent opportunity has its own value case.

A positive tone, a sent document, or seller optimism is never an exit criterion.

## Review and release

Evaluate recipient value, specificity, credibility, reply ease, and naturalness. Hard reject vague research interviews, hidden-context copy, unsupported responsibility, generic software/AI positioning, fabricated collateral, or batch-template reuse.

Keep exact-copy approval for at least the first 30 real GnK messages. At least 24 must pass blind review and be approved unchanged, with zero wrong recipients and zero vague research-interview notes. Promote message/offer motions using positive replies, correct referrals, qualified conversations, paid conversions, delivery results, and customer value—not opens, total replies, or model scores.
