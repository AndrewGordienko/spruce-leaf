# Email and LinkedIn acquisition channels

Status: active policy for founder-reviewed first contact. Spruce Leaf currently generates only email and LinkedIn acquisition copy. Later customer-journey records are handoffs, not automated campaigns.

## One market, different origins

`lead_source` and `lead_temperature` are independent. A conference attendee can be irrelevant; a cold-researched account can be high fit. Every first contact remains bound to a verified opportunity, person, concrete contribution, consent record, and an approved acquisition context.

Supported sources are `cold_email`, `conference`, `webinar`, `referral`, `partner`, and `existing_customer`. Supported first-contact channels are `email`, `linkedin`, and `email_or_linkedin`.

## Cold email

Use verified public evidence, one application, and one recipient-relevant offer. Never imply a prior conversation. Broad coverage means mapping and researching many plausible accounts while holding weak ones; it does not mean repeated sends to the same person.

## Conference

### Before the event

Require a named event, event URL/date, verified exhibitor/attendee/speaker status, ICP fit, and a reason this exact application is relevant. A short request to meet at the named event is allowed. Do not imply familiarity or an agreed meeting.

### After the event

Require actual conversation notes and the agreed next action. The first follow-up references only what was discussed, delivers any promised material, and confirms the recorded next step. It never falls into the cold sequence.

## Webinar

Persist registration separately from attendance, minutes attended, questions, topics viewed, company fit, and later engagement. Registration alone is nurture, not buying intent. A direct follow-up is justified only by a relevant role/company plus a material behavior or question. Copy references only the observed topic or exact question and asks whether a current application exists; it does not pretend education interest is a project.

## Referral and partner

Name the introducer only when recorded and appropriate. Preserve why they believed the application was relevant. Do not turn borrowed trust into manufactured urgency, authority, or a generic cold pitch.

## Existing customer

Start from the recorded relationship and a measured outcome, adoption issue, or independently supported adjacent constraint. The objective is an outcome review or bounded expansion—not re-selling the original product.

## Channel realization

Email uses a specific 3–9 word subject, a natural greeting, the brand word band, one small question, and Andrew's exact signature. LinkedIn has no email subject or signature, stays within 300 characters, and gives a concrete human reason to reply or connect. Three visible candidates must use the approved channel; the writer cannot switch channels to rescue weak context.

## Stops and ownership

A human relationship owner remains visible from first contact onward. A positive reply stops cold automation and creates discovery/application records. No source bypasses identity, evidence, CASL, exact-copy approval, suppression, or controlled-transport gates.
